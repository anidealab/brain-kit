# Pictures, video, sound and 3D

Skills for making and changing pictures, video clips, sound, voices and 3D models with AI models. All are the brain's own: written for this brain, and changed freely. A new skill on this topic gets a line here saying when to use it; a copied one goes under a "Copied" heading below them. The traps agents have hit with renders and screen recordings, beyond these skills: [building and testing](../brain-spec/building-and-testing.md).

## The brain's own
- [fal](../fal/SKILL.md): make or edit a picture, a video clip, audio, a voice or a 3D model with any of the AI models fal.ai hosts, on the owner's own fal key: find a model free, read the price, get the owner's go, run it in the AI job and bring the files back. Also how to set fal up.
- [image-to-3d](../image-to-3d/SKILL.md): turn a picture of one object into a 3D model (GLB), free with TRELLIS.2 on Hugging Face through the AI job, or paid through fal: the picture, cutting its background, the run and what comes back.

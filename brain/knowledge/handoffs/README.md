# Hand-offs

**No new session hand-offs are written:** where a job stands is in its [task note](../tasks/README.md#a-task-note-is-the-jobs-live-status), kept up to date as the work goes, and a fresh chat carries on from that. This shelf is for briefs and reports, and keeps any session hand-offs written before brain update 6 as the record; one that's still open is picked up the way the [handoff skill](../../skills/handoff/SKILL.md#starting-from-a-task-note) says, and its state moves into the job's task note.

One note per hand-off, in the format the [handoff skill](../../skills/handoff/SKILL.md) gives. A task says what the job is and where it stands; a brief carries a piece of it to an agent, and a report brings it back.

- **Two kinds,** in the card's `kind`: `brief` (to an agent) and `report` (back from one). `session` (to a fresh chat) is on notes from before brain update 6.
- **Statuses:** `open` until someone starts from it, `picked-up` while they work from it, `done` once the work is finished or carried on in a newer hand-off. `before` names the hand-off before it, so a chain can be followed back.
- **The board:** the index below lists the open and picked-up ones, newest first. A coordinator reads it, not every hand-off.
- **What's lasting isn't kept here.** Decisions, facts, lessons and tasks go on their shelves first, and a hand-off points to them. The weekly tidy-up folds what's left into the brain, and moves done hand-offs to `history/`, kept as the raw record.
- **File names** start with the date: `{KIT_ISO}-what-it-is.md`.

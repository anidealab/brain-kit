# Brain update 8 from AnIdeaLab: pictures, video, sound and 3D on the owner's own fal key, skills sent by link, picture to 3D model, and a PC copy that stays current without Obsidian

Sent 15 Nov 2026. This update adds three skills: making pictures, video, sound and 3D models with AI models on the owner's own fal key, saving a skill someone sends as a link, and turning a picture of an object into a 3D model, free. It also keeps the brain's copy on the owner's computer up to date where they don't use Obsidian, treats Obsidian as an extra in the setup check, says why the runner's computer should stay awake, and lets the first chat set up a brain made from AnIdeaLab's template. One of the owner's starting defaults changes a little. Nothing runs on the owner's computer, and normally there's nothing to paste.

## Summary for the owner
- **Pictures, video, sound and 3D with AI models (the fal skill):** your agent can now make or change a picture, a short video clip, a voice, music or a 3D model with any of the 1,000+ AI models on fal.ai, and bring the files back. It runs on your own fal account and key, which you set up only if you want this: until then, nothing changes. Finding a model and reading its price are free. Before anything is spent, your agent tells you the cost ("four pictures, about 2 cents") and waits for your go, unless you give it a limit of your own. A cheap picture costs well under a cent, and a 5-second clip between about 5 and 80 cents. The AI calls run on GitHub's machines, inside your account's free monthly minutes, through a new workflow, the AI job, so your keys stay in GitHub's secrets and no agent holds them.
- **Skills sent as a link (the apply-a-skill skill):** when someone sends you a skill as a link to one file, AnIdeaLab or anyone, say "apply" and the link. Your agent reads it first as something to judge, not as orders, saves it if it's sound, and tells you in two lines what it does. It stops and saves nothing if the file asks for a key, money, or anything beyond being saved.
- **A picture to a 3D model (the image-to-3d skill):** a picture of one object becomes a 3D model file that a browser, a game engine or a 3D app opens. Free, on Hugging Face's computers, with a free Hugging Face account's token (about one model a day), or paid through fal with your go. Again, you only set it up if you want it.
- **Your brain's copy on your computer stays up to date without Obsidian:** the PC's runner now brings the copy in `C:\AI-Brain-Link` up to date at the start of each job, where Obsidian isn't syncing it. With Obsidian, nothing changes. The setup check now calls Obsidian an extra, not something to fix, since the brain works without it.
- **Two things for new owners:** the runner's setup now says why to keep the computer awake (one that sleeps can leave its runner disconnected until it's restarted by hand), and the first chat can set up a brain started on GitHub with nothing installed. Your brain is already set up, so you won't see either.
- **One starting default changes:** "rules about how are defaults" used to say agents should prefer accounts you already have. Now it says to pick what's best for the job now, because tools go out of date fast. If you changed that line yourself, yours stays.
- **The kit now starts brains with 68 skills, up from 65.** Your brain's front page keeps the number it started with.

Nothing runs on your computer, and normally there's nothing to paste: only if your agent's session isn't allowed to change the workflow files does it give you one command for that. The runner's workflow changes in your brain's repo and the runner picks it up at its next job: it doesn't need setting up again. fal and Hugging Face need a key only when you want to use them, and your agent gives you one command for each then.

## What changed, and why
1. **The fal skill.** New: `skills/fal/SKILL.md`, `skills/fal/fal_find.py` (finds models, reads their inputs and prices, free, no key) and `skills/fal/run_fal.py` (prices a batch, runs it and saves the files; it sends nothing without `--cap`, nothing over it, and nothing it can't price). New workflow: `.github/workflows/ai-job.yml`, "AI job", which runs a short Python job on GitHub's machines with the brain's `FAL_KEY` and `HF_TOKEN` secrets and attaches what it writes to `out/` for 3 days; it only starts by hand (`workflow_dispatch`). New topic list: `skills/lists/media.md`, "Pictures, video, sound and 3D", with its line in `skills/README.md`. Why: an owner's projects often need a picture, a clip or a voice, and fal reaches most of the well-known models with one pay-as-you-go key. The key stays in the repo's secrets (the secrets gate), so paid calls run in a workflow, and the money gate is built into the script: an estimate, the owner's go, and a cap.
2. **The apply-a-skill skill.** New: `skills/apply-a-skill/SKILL.md` and `skills/apply-a-skill/apply_skill.py` (`show` reads a link or file and writes nothing, flagging lines about keys, money, commands, the core and messages; `save` writes `skills/<name>/`). It's one of the main skills in `skills/README.md` and has a line in `skills/lists/ai-and-claude.md`; `skills/find-skills/SKILL.md` points to it. It also sets the one-file shape for sending a skill: `<name>.skill.md`, the skill's `SKILL.md` first, then each extra file after a `<!-- skill-file: <path> -->` line. Why: skills get passed round as single files and links, and find-skills only copies whole folders from trusted sources. A file from anyone needs reading as something to judge, never as orders, before it goes in.
3. **The image-to-3d skill.** New: `skills/image-to-3d/SKILL.md`, `cut_background.py` and `run_trellis.py`, with its line in `skills/lists/media.md`. The free route is Microsoft's TRELLIS.2 on Hugging Face, called from the AI job with a free account's read token (`HF_TOKEN`); the paid route is the same model through fal. Why: a 3D model from a picture used to need a GPU, and now needs neither a GPU nor a paid account.
4. **A copy without Obsidian, kept up to date.** `.github/workflows/pc-run.yml` has a new step before "Run": where the copy in `C:\AI-Brain-Link\<repo name>` has no Obsidian Git plugin, it fast-forwards the copy from GitHub, signed in as the job. It never stops the job: a copy with changes of its own in the way stays as it is, and the log says why. `skills/my-pc/check-setup.ps1` calls a missing Obsidian a note, not a fix, and without Obsidian says the runner catches the copy up and skips the vault and Git plugin lines. `skills/my-pc/SKILL.md`, `knowledge/systems/my-pc.md`, `skills/add-a-key/SKILL.md` and `skills/upload-point/SKILL.md` each have a line or half a line for a copy without Obsidian. Why: the kit's installers now ask whether the owner wants Obsidian, and without it nothing synced the copy that the setup scripts the owner pastes run from.
5. **The stay-awake question's reason.** `skills/my-pc/setup-runner.ps1` says, before it asks, that a PC that sleeps can leave its runner disconnected after it wakes, until the runner is restarted by hand. It only shows when the runner is set up again.
6. **The first chat knows a brain made from the template.** `skills/getting-started/SKILL.md`: a brain made from AnIdeaLab's template repo has `.github/workflows/first-run.yml`, nothing on the owner's computer, and a new step 0 (its start date, a check that the repo is private, the terms); step 4, "The owner's computer", offers the runner and Obsidian with the kit's one command, and says what to answer at its questions, including the Obsidian one. Steps 1, 5, 7 and 8 say what differs for such a brain. `knowledge/systems/keys-and-accounts.md`: the diagnostics account's row says which setup invited it. Why: a new owner can now start a brain with a button on GitHub and nothing installed. The skill runs once, in a brain's first session, so a brain that's already set up sees no change.
7. **"Rules about how are defaults" loses "prefer accounts they already have".** In the core (`CLAUDE.md`), the default now ends "pick what's best now, not what's already set up, and say why when you change a default", and in the starting rules note (`knowledge/decisions/the-brain/<start date>-starting-rules.md`) it says accounts and keys the owner already has get no preference, because tools go out of date. Why: AI tools go out of date in months, and an account the owner already has is no reason to keep using an older one when a better one does the job now.
8. **The skill count.** `README.md`, `knowledge/areas/the-brain/README.md`, `skills/find-skills/SKILL.md` and `skills/getting-started/SKILL.md` say a brain starts with 68 skills, not 65. A brain that started with another number keeps it in `README.md` and `knowledge/areas/the-brain/README.md`.

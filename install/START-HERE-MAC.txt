Your own second brain, started from the AnIdeaLab brain kit, on a Mac.

THE QUICKEST WAY NEEDS NOTHING INSTALLED: three steps in your browser, about ten minutes, and a
phone will do. They're here: https://github.com/AnIdeaLab/brain-kit#start-a-brain
This page is the other way: starting from your Mac. It's also how you add Obsidian and the Mac
runner to a brain you've already made: every brain is called my-brain-ail, and the setup finds
yours. A brain made before 22 Nov 2026 has another name: ask its chat for its command, which
has its name in it.

What you'll have at the end:
- Your brain: a private GitHub repo of plain notes. A core page every AI agent reads, 68 skills
  (how to do things) and a knowledge library that fills up as you use it.
- Obsidian on your Mac, if you'd like it, showing your brain as a map, and the brain graph
  replaying how it grew.
- The Mac runner, if you'd like it: then you can ask Claude to do things on your Mac from any
  chat, even your phone, while it's on and you're logged in. It only does what you ask.

You need: a Mac, a Claude plan, and about 30 minutes, mostly waiting for installs.

1. OPEN TERMINAL: press Command and Space, type terminal, press Enter.

2. PASTE THIS LINE into the Terminal window and press Enter:

/bin/bash -c "$(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AnIdeaLab/brain-kit/main/install.sh)"

   It installs Apple's command line tools (they have Git) and GitHub's command line, if they're
   missing, and Obsidian if you say yes to it. It doesn't need your Mac's password. Its
   questions show in yellow.
   It asks you to:
   - accept AnIdeaLab's terms of use: type Y and press Enter. Read them first, here or by typing
     V in the setup: https://github.com/AnIdeaLab/brain-kit/blob/main/TERMS.md
   - say whether you'd like Obsidian (press Enter for yes). Your brain works without it;
   - if a window asks to install the command line developer tools: click Install, then Agree.
     It takes 5 to 20 minutes, and the setup carries on by itself once they're in;
   - sign in to GitHub in your browser, copying a code from the window (no account yet? make
     one on that page, it's free);
   - say yes to the Mac runner. macOS then says background items were added: that's the runner;
   - say yes to keeping the Mac awake while it's on the charger. Most people say no here, and
     then your brain can't reach the Mac whenever it has gone to sleep. The screen still turns off;
   - Terminal then lists each step and what to do next. If you chose Obsidian, it's
     installed and set up, but not opened yet: Claude tells you how in the next step.

   If it stops partway, paste the same line again: it skips what's already done. What it
   did is kept in ~/AI-Brain-Link/_logs/install.log.

3. CONNECT CLAUDE:
   - Go to https://claude.ai/code. It needs a paid plan (Pro or above). If it asks, connect GitHub and let Claude see your brain repo.
   - Start a new session with your brain repo selected, and send:

       This is my new second brain. Read CLAUDE.md and get me set up.

   Claude asks you a few questions, tests the Mac runner and shows you around, with how
   to open your brain in Obsidian if you chose it.

Later:
- Agents can run jobs on your Mac while it's on, awake and you're logged in. Closing the lid
  puts it to sleep, and so does running on battery.
- Keys for services never go in a chat. Claude gives you one line to paste into Terminal,
  which opens the page that makes the key and saves it in your brain's GitHub secrets.
- To check your setup any time, paste this into Terminal. It changes nothing, and copies a
  report you can paste into Claude with "Fix what this setup check found":

bash ~/AI-Brain-Link/my-brain-ail/skills/my-mac/check-setup.sh

  (A brain made before 22 Nov 2026 has its own name: put it in place of my-brain-ail.)

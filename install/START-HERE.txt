Your own second brain, started from the AnIdeaLab brain kit.

THE QUICKEST WAY NEEDS NOTHING INSTALLED: three steps in your browser, and a phone
will do. They're here: https://github.com/AnIdeaLab/brain-kit#start-a-brain
This page is the other way: starting from your Windows PC. It's also how you add Obsidian and
the PC runner to a brain you've already made: every brain is called my-brain-ail, and the
setup finds yours. A brain made before 22 Nov 2026 has another name: ask its chat for its
command, which has its name in it.

What you'll have at the end:
- Your brain: a private GitHub repo of plain notes. A core page every AI agent reads, 68 skills
  (how to do things) and a knowledge library that fills up as you use it.
- Obsidian on your PC, if you'd like it, showing your brain as a map, and the brain graph
  replaying how it grew.
- The PC runner, if you'd like it: then you can ask Claude to do things on your PC from any
  chat, even your phone. It only does what you ask.

You need: a Windows PC, a Claude plan, and about 30 minutes, mostly waiting for installs.

1. OPEN POWERSHELL: press the Windows key, type powershell, press Enter.

2. PASTE THIS LINE into the blue window and press Enter:

irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AnIdeaLab/brain-kit/main/install.ps1 | iex

   It installs Git and GitHub's command line, and Obsidian if you say yes to it, and Windows
   may ask you to allow each. Its questions show in yellow. It asks you to:
   - accept AnIdeaLab's terms of use: type Y and press Enter. Read them first, here or by typing
     V in the setup: https://github.com/AnIdeaLab/brain-kit/blob/main/TERMS.md
   - say whether you'd like Obsidian (press Enter for yes). Your brain works without it;
   - sign in to GitHub in your browser, copying a code from the window (no account yet? make
     one on that page, it's free);
   - say yes to the PC runner, so Claude can do things on your PC when you ask. It needs no
     password, and starts by itself whenever you sign in to Windows;
   - say whether to keep the PC awake while it's plugged in, so Claude can do what you ask when
     you're away from it. The screen still turns off;
   - the window then lists each step and what to do next. If you chose Obsidian, it's
     installed and set up, but not opened yet: Claude tells you how in the next step.

   If it stops partway, paste the same line again: it skips what's already done. What it
   did is kept in C:\AI-Brain-Link\_logs\install.log.

3. CONNECT CLAUDE:
   - Go to https://claude.ai/code. It needs a paid plan (Pro or above). If it asks, connect GitHub and let Claude see your brain repo.
   - Start a new session with your brain repo selected, and send:

       This is my new second brain. Read CLAUDE.md and get me set up.

   Claude asks you a few questions, tests the PC runner and shows you around, with how
   to open your brain in Obsidian if you chose it.

Later:
- Keys for services never go in a chat. Claude tells you the name, and you add the key on
  GitHub: your brain repo, then Settings, Secrets and variables, Actions, New repository secret.
- To check your setup any time, paste this into PowerShell. It changes nothing, and copies a
  report you can paste into Claude with "Fix what this setup check found":

powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File "C:\AI-Brain-Link\my-brain-ail\skills\my-pc\check-setup.ps1"

  (A brain made before 22 Nov 2026 has its own name: put it in place of my-brain-ail.)
